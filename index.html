How can we help you today?

Money debited but not credited to recipient? expand_more
UPI transactions are instant. In rare banking server delays, money is automatically settled or refunded to your bank account within 24 to 48 hours according to RBI guidelines.
How do I change or reset my UPI PIN? expand_more
Go to Profile > Payment Methods > Select your Bank Account > Click 'Reset UPI PIN'. Enter the last 6 digits of your debit card and expiry date, then enter the OTP to set a new PIN.
How to report an unauthorized or fraud transaction? expand_more
Call our 24x7 helpline 080-68727374 immediately or block UPI access via your bank. PhonePe will never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP, or passwords.
Why did my payment fail due to technical error? expand_more
Payment failures usually occur due to temporary bank server downtime, insufficient balance, or entering an incorrect UPI PIN. Any deducted money will be reversed automatically.
